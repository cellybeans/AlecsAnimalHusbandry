# Alec's Animal Husbandry 2.0 Promotional Image

## Goal

Create a cinematic promotional image for the 2.0 update that makes the Frost
Dragon the immediate hero while clearly communicating the update's other key
features.

## Source and invariants

- Use `HytaleClient_5ktmNt1AHT.jpg` as the background.
- Do not alter any screenshot pixels. Generate and composite all new elements
  as transparent overlays.
- Keep the Frost Dragon visually unobstructed on the right side of the image.

## Layout

### Title

Place a restrained, icy Hytale-style pixel-art title in the upper-left:

- `Alec's Animal Husbandry v2.0`
- `Wings of the Frozen North`

The title uses snowy mountain materials: frosted white and pale blue faces,
navy pixel shadows, and small snow/ice details. It should remain smaller and
quieter than the previous title explorations, leaving the dragon as the focal
point.

### Feature ribbon

Add one semi-transparent, frosted navy ribbon across the lower-left. Divide it
into three equal callout cards with compact high-contrast pixel lettering:

1. `Dragonriding` — an icy wing or mounted-dragon emblem.
2. `Flying Companions` — a small flock or feather emblem.
3. `Saddles & Blankets` — an emblem that incorporates the real AH brown leather
   saddle and cyan wool blanket material.

Use pale icy borders and restrained snow accents. The ribbon must describe the
new features without competing with the main title or dragon.
