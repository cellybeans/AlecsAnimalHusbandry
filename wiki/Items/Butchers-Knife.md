# Butcher's Knife

Parent: [Items Index](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/items-index)

Hold primary use on an owned, tamed animal to cull it. The knife uses the
normal death path, so normal drops and death effects still occur.

The knife does not cull bonded companion projections. It has no recipe or
durability cost yet. Its Scrap Sword model and icon are temporary placeholders.
